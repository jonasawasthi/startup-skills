# Executive summary

Fieldnote is a fictional company used to demonstrate this skill. The beachhead is mid-size dairy cooperatives in the pilot region, where 14 cooperatives use the field app weekly (company-reported) [E-001] and 5 pay USD 40 a month (company-reported) [E-007]. The plan concentrates on two channels: founder-led sales, and partnerships with regional cooperative federations. The 90-day objective is a measured pilot-to-paid conversion and the first retention cohort.

> [!KEY] Known: weekly pilot usage [E-001], paying pilots and price (reported) [E-007]. Assumed: churn [E-009] is a planning assumption, and the annual software budget per cooperative [E-004] is an untested assumption. Gross margin and acquisition spend have not been measured.

# 6. Customer acquisition strategy

| Rank | Channel | Evidence score | Total | Decision |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Founder-led sales [E-001] | 4 | 4.20 | primary |
| 2 | Partnerships | 1 | 3.35 | primary |
| 3 | Product-led growth | 1 | 2.70 | test later: needs self-serve onboarding |
| 4 | Paid advertising | 1 | 2.50 | test later: low intent, no retention evidence |

**Founder-led sales.** Managers buy from people they trust. The experiment: visit 20 cooperatives over 8 weeks with a USD 3,000 budget. Continue if at least 5 start a paid pilot, and stop if fewer than 2 do.

# 11. KPI dashboard

| Metric | Value | Status | Formula |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Purchase conversion | 35.7% | reported | new customers ÷ activated |
| Churn (period) | 2.0% | assumption | supplied as a planning assumption |

LTV, CAC and payback are **not computed**: gross margin and acquisition spend are missing inputs. The KPI workbook tracks them weekly from the first 30 days.

# 13. Risks, assumptions and validation plan

| Assumption | Ledger ID | Status | Test | Kill signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperatives can afford the price | E-004 | assumption | Annual pre-pay offer to 5 pilots | 0 prepayments in 6 weeks |
| Churn stays low | E-009 | assumption | Cohort retention report at day 90 | More than 1 of 5 paying pilots cancels |
