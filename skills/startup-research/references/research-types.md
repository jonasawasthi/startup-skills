# Research types and query patterns

| Type | Key questions | Primary sources to try first | Query patterns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industry research | Structure, value chain, growth, power | Statistics offices, industry associations, regulators, listed-company filings | `"<industry>" annual report <country> <year>`, `site:<gov domain> <industry> statistics` |
| Competitor intelligence | Offer, pricing, segment, traction claims, funding, recent moves | Competitor websites, pricing pages, app-store listings, changelogs, press releases, filings | `"<competitor>" pricing`, `"<competitor>" raises`, `site:<competitor domain> changelog` |
| Customer research | Segments, behaviours, needs, willingness to pay | Interviews (your own), public surveys with methodology, community forums (as qualitative signal) | `"<role>" survey <topic> <year> methodology` |
| Market sizing inputs | Counts of customers, spend, prices | Registries, census or statistics releases, regulator reports, platform transparency reports | `number of <entities> registered <country> <year>` |
| Geographic analysis | Payments, connectivity, language, regulation, diaspora | Central bank, telecom regulator, statistics office, migration statistics | `site:<central bank> payment systems report`, `<country> diaspora population statistics` |
| Business-model benchmarking | Take rates, margins, retention norms | Public company filings, published benchmarks with methodology ([SaaS](../../../knowledge/other/cloud-and-saas.md)) | `"take rate" <category> 10-K`, `<category> gross margin annual report` |
| Pricing research | Price points, packaging, local purchasing power | Pricing pages (record the date), app stores, local competitors | `"<category>" pricing <country>` |
| Technology landscape | Build vs buy, vendors, maturity, costs | Official docs, pricing pages, release notes | `<vendor> pricing API`, `<technology> production case study` |
| Regulatory research | Licences, data protection, payments, tax, FDI, content | Statute and regulator sites, official gazettes, central bank circulars | `site:<regulator domain> <topic> directive <year>` |
| Opportunity discovery | Underserved problems, recent changes, proxies from other markets | Regulatory changes, new platforms, successful companies elsewhere | "recently became possible" checklist from [idea evaluation](../../../knowledge/yc/idea-evaluation.md) |

For any cross-border question, also work through the [emerging-markets checklist](../../../knowledge/frameworks/emerging-markets.md).
