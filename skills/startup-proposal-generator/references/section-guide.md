# Section guide

| # | Section | What good looks like | Evidence and tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover page | Title, subtitle, company, date, version, confidentiality, reader | Front matter |
| 2 | Executive summary | One page: the ask/decision, what the company does, why it matters, proof, plan, risks. Written last | All |
| 3 | Company overview | Legal entity, location, founding date, stage, products in one table (with status) | profile.yaml, products.md |
| 4 | Vision and mission | The five-year picture (Sequoia "vision") and the day-to-day purpose. 2 short paragraphs | company.md |
| 5 | Problem statement | The customer's pain, quantified. Current workarounds. Who suffers most | problem.md, interviews |
| 6 | Market opportunity | Definition, bottom-up sizing with the calculator table, why now, trends with dated sources | market-size |
| 7 | Target customers | ICP, personas (user vs buyer), early adopters, buying process | customers.md |
| 8 | Products and services | Each product: job, users, capabilities, **status**, pricing | products.md, codebase inventory |
| 9 | Product architecture | How the products fit together. Shared services (identity, payments, data). A dependency table | scan, diagram |
| 10 | Value proposition | Per segment: the gain over the status quo, quantified where possible | interviews, pricing |
| 11 | Business model | Who pays, for what, and how. Margin drivers | business-model.md |
| 12 | Revenue strategy | Streams, sequencing by product, pricing logic, expansion revenue | model.yaml |
| 13 | Competitive analysis | Four layers of alternatives, a matrix, dated sources | competitors.md |
| 14 | Market positioning | Where we win and why. Where we deliberately don't compete | |
| 15 | Go-to-market strategy | Beachhead, first 100 customers, channels with CAC hypotheses | |
| 16 | Marketing and distribution | Channel plan, partnerships, community, content, budget | |
| 17 | Operational strategy | How delivery works: support, payouts, onboarding, quality, compliance ops | |
| 18 | Technology and infrastructure | Stack, hosting, security, data protection, scalability, key vendors | scan |
| 19 | Roadmap and milestones | Dated milestones with statuses and dependencies. What funding unlocks | roadmap.md |
| 20 | Team and organisation | Founders, key roles, hiring plan, advisors (only confirmed) | team.md |
| 21 | Financial assumptions | Table of every model input with status and evidence | XLSX Assumptions sheet |
| 22 | Financial projections | Annual summary, scenarios, runway / default-alive test. Labelled as scenarios | model summary JSON |
| 23 | Funding requirements | Amount (founder-decided), instrument, timing, prior funding | profile.yaml |
| 24 | Use of funds | Allocation table tied to milestones | |
| 25 | Risk assessment | Register across 8 categories with mitigations | risk-register |
| 26 | Long-term opportunities | Expansion markets and products, sequenced, clearly labelled as future | |
| 27 | Conclusion | The decision requested and the next steps, with dates | |
| 28 | References and appendices | Auto-generated sources table, plus methodology, glossary and detailed tables | ledger |
