# Proposal types

| Type (`doc_type`) | Reader and decision | Theme | Length | Emphasise | Usually omit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| General startup proposal (`proposal`) | Mixed stakeholders: understand and support | venture | 15–30 pp | Clear narrative, products, model, roadmap | Deep financial tables |
| Investor proposal (`investor-proposal`) | Investor: diligence and invest | venture | 20–40 pp | Market, traction, unit economics, financial scenarios, use of funds, risks | Operational minutiae |
| Strategic partnership (`partnership-proposal`) | Partner exec: approve a partnership | corporate | 8–20 pp | Mutual value, integration plan, pilot design, governance, KPIs, commercial terms options | Fundraising |
| Accelerator application (`accelerator-application`) | Selection committee: admit | accelerator | Their form | Clarity, progress pace, founder–market fit, insight (see [YC application](../../../knowledge/yc/application.md)) | Long prose |
| Government / grant (`grant-proposal`) | Programme officer: fund against criteria | institutional | Their template | Public benefit, eligibility, impact metrics, budget with justification, compliance, sustainability after the grant, M&E plan | Hype, valuation talk |
| Corporate collaboration (`corporate-proposal`) | Business unit: co-develop or procure | corporate | 8–15 pp | Problem in their terms, solution fit, security and compliance, pilot, pricing | Investor framing |
| Internal business plan (`business-plan`) | The team or board: align and execute | internal | 10–25 pp | Goals, priorities, owners, budget, milestones, metrics, risks | Persuasive framing |
