# Writing style for proposals

- **Answer first.** Each section opens with its conclusion, followed by the support.
- **Plain, specific language.** Prefer "Creators keep 90% of membership revenue [E-014]" over "creator-first monetisation".
- **Paragraphs carry reasoning.** Use bullets for lists of parallel items, never for arguments.
- **Numbers carry context:** base, period, definition and source.
- **Qualify assumptions in the same sentence**: "We assume…", "Our base scenario projects…", "If pre-sales confirm…". The build warns when an assumption is cited without qualifiers.
- **Tables for comparison**: competitors, scenarios, budgets, risks.
- **No superlatives without evidence** ("leading", "revolutionary", "unique", "first").
- **Consistent terms.** Define product names and roles once, then use them consistently.
- **Grant and government readers**: mirror the criteria's language, give measurable outcomes, and include a monitoring and evaluation plan.
- **Partner readers**: write in their terms (their KPIs, their customers, their risks).
