# Deck design rules

The engine enforces most of these. The rest need judgement when you review the previews.

1. **One idea per slide.** If you need "and" in the title, split the slide.
2. **Action titles**: full sentences, ≤ 2 lines, Georgia (venture/angel) or Arial (other themes).
3. **Hierarchy**: kicker (small caps, accent) → title → content → source line → footer. Don't add extra levels.
4. **Minimal text**: ≤ 70 words of slide text. Use notes for detail.
5. **One accent colour.** Use the secondary accent only for a deliberate contrast. Status colours are reserved for status.
6. **White space is structure.** Don't fill empty areas with decoration.
7. **Real product imagery.** Screenshots beat illustrations. Never use stock or AI-generated people or "dashboard" art.
8. **Charts**: show values on the marks, no gridlines, highlight the point that matters, put the source under the chart.
9. **Consistent alignment**: every element snaps to the 0.75" margins and the content band.
10. **Brand**: use the startup's accent and logo when they exist. Fonts in PPTX must be installed on viewers' machines, so prefer the defaults unless the brand font is common.

## Review the previews against these questions

- Can each slide be understood in 5 seconds?
- Is the most important number the largest thing on the slide?
- Are there orphaned words, cramped boxes or text touching edges?
- Do the "Assumption", "Estimate" and "Reported" pills appear where expected?
- Does the deck look like it belongs to *this* company (brand, product screenshots), or like a template?
