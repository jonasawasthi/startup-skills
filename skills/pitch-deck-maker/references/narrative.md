# Narrative selection

Read [deck narratives](../../../knowledge/frameworks/deck-narratives.md) and [investor storytelling](../../../knowledge/sequoia/investor-storytelling.md) first.

## Decide three things

1. **Stage** → slide-count range (idea 6–10, pre-seed 8–12, seed 10–15, Series A 12–20, growth 12–22).
2. **Audience** → theme and emphasis (VC, angel, accelerator, corporate, government, internal).
3. **Evidence profile** → slide order:

| Profile | Order |
|---|---|
| Traction-led | cover → problem → solution → traction → insight → product → model → market → competition → team → ask → closing |
| Thesis-led | cover → purpose → problem → why now → solution → product → market → competition → model → team → ask → closing |
| Technology-led | cover → problem → insight → technology (diagram) → milestones de-risked → market → model → team → ask |
| Ecosystem wedge | cover → problem → wedge product → evidence on wedge → ecosystem map (statuses) → model → market → competition → roadmap → team → ask |

## The 22-slide menu

Cover · vision · problem · why now · solution · product · demo (screenshots) · customers and use cases · market opportunity · market sizing · business model · traction · go-to-market · competition · competitive advantage · technology and defensibility · roadmap · team · financial outlook · fundraising ask · use of funds · closing vision.

Merge slides wherever possible: vision + closing, market opportunity + sizing, competition + advantage, ask + use of funds. **Don't force all 22.**

## Outline test (before writing the spec)

Read only the titles in order. They should tell the whole story. If a title is a label ("Market"), rewrite it as a claim ("A $X beachhead, sized bottom-up").

## Multi-product ecosystems

Investors fund focus. Lead with **one wedge** (the product with the most evidence), then show the ecosystem as the expansion path with honest statuses. A slide full of planned products reads as a lack of focus, so keep planned items to the roadmap.
