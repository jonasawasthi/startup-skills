# Negotiation preparation (informational; not legal advice)

## Dilution maths

- **Post-money SAFE**: investor ownership at conversion ≈ investment / post-money valuation cap.
- **Priced round**: new investor ownership = investment / post-money valuation; existing holders are diluted by the same proportion.
- **Option pool** created pre-money dilutes the founders, not the new investors. Ask where the pool sits.

Example: $500K on a $5M post-money cap SAFE → ~10% at conversion (before later rounds' dilution).

## Term checklist

| Term | What to understand | Founder-friendly default to discuss with counsel |
|---|---|---|
| Valuation / cap | Pre vs post-money | Know which one is quoted |
| Discount | Conversion discount on SAFEs or notes | Typical ranges vary by market. Get local comparables |
| Pro rata rights | Right to invest in future rounds | Common for leads |
| MFN | Most-favoured-nation clause | Understand its scope |
| Liquidation preference | 1× non-participating is common in many markets | Avoid participating or multiple preferences |
| Board composition | Control | Founder control at seed is common |
| Information rights | Reporting obligations | Reasonable cadence |
| Vesting and acceleration | Founder vesting reset | Negotiate credit for time served |
| Jurisdiction and instrument | Local recognition of SAFEs, FDI approvals | Check with local counsel early |

## Preparation

1. Know your walk-away: the minimum amount, maximum dilution, and the terms you won't accept.
2. Know your alternatives (other term sheets, default-alive status) and keep them truthful.
3. Don't negotiate in real time on calls (Ralston). Take terms away and reply in writing.
4. Never play investors off against each other with false information.
