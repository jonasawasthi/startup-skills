# Outreach

## Principles

- Personalise with one specific, verifiable reason for the fit (a portfolio company, stated thesis or talk), and cite it in your notes.
- Lead with what the company does and the strongest *true* proof point.
- Keep it short: 90–150 words. One clear ask (a 20–30 minute call).
- Attach or link the short deck, not the full deck.
- No fake urgency ("closing Friday" when it isn't), no implied endorsements, no "X introduced us" without X's consent.

## Templates

**Warm-intro request (to the connector)**
> Subject: Intro to <investor>?
>
> Hi <name>, would you be comfortable introducing me to <investor>? <Company> <does X for Y>. <One proof point with a date>. I think it's relevant to them because <specific reason>. A forwardable note is below. No worries at all if it's not a fit.

**Forwardable note / cold email**
> Subject: <Company>: <what you do in 6–8 words>
>
> Hi <investor>, I'm <name>, founder of <Company>. We <do X for Y, instead of Z>. <Proof point: metric, as of date>. We're raising <round> to <milestone>. I'm reaching out because <specific, verifiable reason>. Would you be open to a 20-minute call? Short deck: <link>.

**Follow-up after a meeting (within 24 hours)**
> Thanks for your time today. As promised: <answers to the open questions, with evidence>. <One material update, if any>. Next step we discussed: <step and date>.

**Monthly update to prospects** → use the [investor-update template](../../business-document-generator/assets/templates/investor-update.md).

## Pre-send check

Every number in the email must match the ledger (verified or reported, with a date). Remove anything that doesn't.
