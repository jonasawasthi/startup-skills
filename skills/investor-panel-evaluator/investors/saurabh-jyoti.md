# Saurabh Jyoti

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Saurabh Jyoti chairs Syakar Trading Company, the Honda distributor in Nepal, within his family's Padma Jyoti Group, which was called the Jyoti Group until December 2024 [S8][S6][S9]. He joined the group's steel business in 1999 and worked across manufacturing and trading before leading Syakar [S6]. In a 2020 interview he described after-sales service as the core of the Honda business, a long-term test for investing in local assembly, and a policy of retraining staff [S6]; by 2025 his company was assembling Honda motorcycles in Nepal [S10]. He has led automobile dealer and young-entrepreneur associations and chaired FNCCI committees [S6][S10].

## Panel membership

- Shark Tank Nepal: seasons 1–2, current (season 2 announced on 28 September 2026 with the same five Sharks) [S2][S3][S4].
