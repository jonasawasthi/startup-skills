## Summary

Susie Ma founded Tropic Skincare at 15 from a London market stall and runs it as chief executive [S1, S8]. After she reached the final stages of The Apprentice in 2011, Lord Sugar invested in Tropic as a partner; she bought out his stake in 2023 and is now sole owner [S1, S8]. Tropic sells through a network of self-employed Ambassadors rather than traditional retail, and the BBC gives its annual turnover as £68 million [S1, S8]. In the BBC's series 23 media pack she says the entrepreneur matters most and that she looks for clear numbers and real-world proof [S3]. Her first Den deal, in Cubbi, was reported as completed in February 2026 [S9].
