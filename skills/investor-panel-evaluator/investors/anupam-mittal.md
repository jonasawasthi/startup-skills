# Anupam Mittal

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Anupam Mittal founded Shaadi.com, an online matchmaking service that began as sagaai.com, after working at MicroStrategy in the United States [S7, S13]. He is founder and chief executive of People Group, whose businesses have included Makaan.com, Mauj Mobile and People Pictures [S8, S9]. He is also an angel investor; Entrepreneur India put his count at around 225 startups in 2022, which is not independently verified [S8]. He has described total addressable market, team, timing and later traction as his main early-stage criteria [S8, S9]. In 2023 he publicly set out why programme deals take months to complete and why some fall through [S10, S11].
