# Kunal Bahl

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Kunal Bahl co-founded Snapdeal (now AceVector Limited), where he is a whole-time director, and is a promoter and non-executive director of the listed e-commerce software company Unicommerce [S7]. With Rohit Bansal he co-founded Titan Capital, an early-stage investment office whose follow-on Winners Fund closed at INR 333 crore in 2024 [S8, S11]. He studied at the Wharton School and the University of Pennsylvania and chairs the CII start-up council [S7]. In a signed 2023 article he argued that unit economics need attention from launch and that scale rarely fixes them [S9]. He has also said cash burn is acceptable when its purpose is clear [S10].
