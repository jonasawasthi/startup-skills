# Peyush Bansal

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Peyush Bansal is co-founder, promoter and chief executive of Lenskart, an eyewear company that was incorporated in 2008 as Valyoo Technologies and listed on Indian exchanges in November 2025 [S9, S10]. He left Microsoft in the United States to start the company [S13]. Lenskart sells through online channels and about 2,600 stores and runs its own manufacturing [S10, S12]. Inc42 reported FY25 net profit of INR 297.3 crore [S10]. Around the IPO he described the listing as a beginning rather than an exit and the business as eye-care infrastructure for a billion people [S11, S12].
