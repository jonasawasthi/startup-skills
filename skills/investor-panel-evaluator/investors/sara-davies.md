# Sara Davies

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Sara Davies founded Crafter's Companion, a craft tools business, in 2005 while at the University of York [S10, S1]. The company designs and manufactures many of its own products and sells through channels including the HSN and QVC shopping networks [S1, S10]. In January 2025 the company entered administration and its business was sold to entities jointly owned by Davies and Modella Capital, with Davies returning as chief executive [S7, S8]. In August 2025 Maven Capital Partners took Modella's place alongside an increased stake for Davies, who moved to a non-executive board role [S9]. On her own site she says the founder matters more to her than the idea [S2].

## Panel membership

- Dragons' Den (BBC): series 17 (2019) to series 22 (2025); former [S1, S3, S5, S6]. She described the step away as for now [S3].
