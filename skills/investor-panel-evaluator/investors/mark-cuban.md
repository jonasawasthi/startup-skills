# Mark Cuban

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Mark Cuban started MicroSolutions with an advance from his first customer [S4] and was chairman and president of Broadcast.com when Yahoo agreed to acquire it in 1999 [S6]. He founded the Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company, an online pharmacy with a published cost-plus price formula [S7] [S8], sold his majority stake in the Dallas Mavericks in 2023 and co-founded Harbinger Sports Partners in 2025 [S9]. On his blog in 2008 he argued that raising cash early is often a mistake, that sales solve most problems and that founders should be obsessed with their company [S4] [S5].
