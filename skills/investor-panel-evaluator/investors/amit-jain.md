# Amit Jain

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Amit Jain is co-founder and chief executive of the CarDekho Group, whose parent GirnarSoft he started with his brother Anurag Jain in Jaipur as a software outsourcing company [S1, S9]. An IIT Delhi alumnus, he worked as a software engineer in the United States first [S1, S11]. CarDekho.com went live in 2008, was bootstrapped for about seven years and grew through content and search before raising a Series A in 2013 [S1, S8, S9]. The group added insurance distribution (InsuranceDekho), vehicle lending (Rupyy) and Southeast Asian sites [S1, S8, S9]. He has said he puts the founder before the business and favours frugal growth to product-market fit [S11].
