## Summary

He co-founded Storage Now, sold in 2007, and leads O'Leary Financial Group, which includes O'Shares Investment Advisers and O'Leary Ventures [S2]. In interviews he expects free cash flow within three years [S6], limits debt service to about a third of free cash flow [S7] [S8], requires acquisition cost below lifetime value [S7], and looks for focused founders who execute [S9].
