## Summary

Vineeta Singh co-founded SUGAR Cosmetics in 2015 with Kaushik Mukherjee and is its chief executive, after an earlier beauty subscription venture that closed [S7]. SUGAR began online and expanded into offline retail, which she said made up about 60 percent of its business in 2025 [S7, S8]. She has said the company would not acquire customers at a loss and that offline presence builds trust that supports online sales [S7]. Inc42 reported in September 2026 that SUGAR's FY25 revenue fell and losses grew, attributed mainly to offline expansion, and that it raised new capital from A91 Partners in 2026 [S9]. A 2023 analysis of season 1 filings found she completed 40 percent of her on-air commitments [S10].
