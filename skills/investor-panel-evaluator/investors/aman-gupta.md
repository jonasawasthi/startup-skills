# Aman Gupta

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Aman Gupta co-founded Imagine Marketing, the company behind the boAt consumer electronics brand, with Sameer Mehta; the company was incorporated in 2013 [S7]. Its 2022 draft prospectus listed him as executive director and chief marketing officer and reported that most revenue came through online marketplaces, with an offline distributor network alongside [S7]. In September 2025 Inc42 reported that he would continue as a non-executive director when a new chief executive was appointed, and in October 2025 he was named as a selling shareholder in the updated IPO filing [S8, S9]. He is a chartered accountant and previously worked at KPMG India, Harman International India and Citicorp Finance India [S7]. In public remarks he has stressed product-market fit before spending and lean early operations [S10, S11].
