## Summary

Davie Fogarty founded Davie Group, which Paramount ANZ described in 2023 as running 10 consumer brands including The Oodie [S1]. After several failed early ventures he launched Calming Blankets and then The Oodie, a direct-to-consumer brand [S3, S4]. SmartCompany reported group revenue of $182 million for FY2020-21, with the group solely owned and without outside investors at the time [S3]; later sales figures are his own claims and differ by measure [S4, S5]. He mentors established ecommerce brands through Daily Mentor [S2, S4, S5]. A 2023 trade report records a $250,000 investment in the suit-hire business Suitor [S6].
