## Summary

Namita Thapar is a whole-time director and one of the promoters of Emcure Pharmaceuticals, which filed a red herring prospectus for its IPO in June 2024; she joined the company in 2006 and was previously its chief financial officer [S7]. She is a chartered accountant with an MBA from Duke's Fuqua School of Business, worked at Guidant Corporation in the United States, and later ran Emcure's India business [S7, S8]. She founded Incredible Ventures, which teaches entrepreneurship to young people [S8]. Her book The Dolphin and the Shark sets out what she considers a strong pitch [S10]. A 2023 analysis of season 1 filings found she completed the highest share of her on-air commitments [S11].
