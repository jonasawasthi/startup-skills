# Anand Bagaria

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-14.

## Summary

Anand Bagaria has led the growth of Nimbus, a Nepali agribusiness group that Nepali Times described in 2008 as family-run, from poultry feed supplements into pellet feed manufacturing, and later into agri-processing, packaged food and an online grocery store [S5][S6][S7]. Business press from 2008 and 2011 records his own account of how the feed business won farmer customers and built rural distribution through dealers and depots [S5][S6]. In a 2022 interview he described running Nimbus Holdings through the pandemic, his view of e-commerce logistics costs and a shift to smaller, dispersed plants [S7]. A 2024 report names him as a director of Nimbus in a poultry-sector partnership [S8]. The broadcaster's biography adds a Tootle investment and climate non-profit work that could not be confirmed elsewhere [S1].

## Panel membership

- Shark Tank Nepal: seasons 1–2, current (season 2 announced on 28 September 2026 with the same five Sharks) [S2][S3][S4].
