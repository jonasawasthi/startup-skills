## Summary

Barbara Corcoran borrowed $1,000 to start a New York City real estate company that became The Corcoran Group [S2] [S3], and sold her company in 2001 [S8]. She is an investor and executive producer on ABC's Shark Tank, a keynote speaker and the author of Shark Tales [S2] [S3]. In interviews she says she backs the founder more than the product, looking for ambition and a clear vision [S5] [S6], and that accountability for problems predicts success [S8]. She has said that only about one in ten of her programme investments has made her money [S6].
