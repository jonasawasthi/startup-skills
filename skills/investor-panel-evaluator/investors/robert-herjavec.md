> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Robert Herjavec founded BRAK Systems in 1990 and sold it to AT&T Canada in 2000 [S9]. He founded Herjavec Group (2003 per ABC and his own account), now the cybersecurity company Cyderes, and leads global strategy at Zetaris, where he is a minority investor [S2] [S9]. He has written that founders should be able to run every part of their own business [S7], and in interviews says he backs founders over products, looks for scale and distinct offers [S8] [S10], and checks resilience and whether the numbers support the story [S4].
