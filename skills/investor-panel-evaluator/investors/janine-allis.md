> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Janine Allis founded Boost Juice, opening the first store in South Australia in 2000 [S6, S8]. Boost grew into Retail Zoo, a multi-brand franchise group that Bain Capital and the founders acquired in 2014 [S6]. In 2023 Bain sold its stake to Adamantem Capital, and Inside Retail reported that she would stay as a shareholder and director [S7]. In interviews she has said she looks closely at the person behind the product [S4, S5], and that a business must be financially stable before it franchises [S3]. Her own site promotes a business academy, a podcast and speaking [S8].
