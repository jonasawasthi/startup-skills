## Summary

Ashneer Grover was co-founder and managing director of the merchant payments company BharatPe until he resigned in March 2022 [S1, S4]. He was on the season 1 panel of Shark Tank India and was reported in November 2022 to have been dropped from season 2 [S2, S3]. In January 2023 he founded Third Unicorn, whose fantasy cricket app CrickPe shut in late 2024 and whose lending products ZeroPe and Fund My Staff work through registered NBFCs [S8, S9, S10]. In a 2023 interview he said he would avoid venture money with strings attached and compete through product rather than spending [S8].
