# Steve Baxter

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Steve Baxter founded the internet provider SE Net in 1994 and co-founded PIPE Networks, which TPG acquired in 2010 [S2, S7]. He has invested in early-stage startups through Transition Level Investments since 2010, co-founded and chairs TEN13, and leads Beaten Zone Venture Partners in defence technology [S1, S2, S3]. He founded the Brisbane hub River City Labs and served as Queensland's Chief Entrepreneur [S7]. In 2017 interviews he said every deal comes down to the people, the problem and the approach, and that customer traction outweighs opinion [S4, S5].

- Shark Tank Australia: seasons 1–4 (2015–2018), former [S1, S2, S8, S9, S10].
