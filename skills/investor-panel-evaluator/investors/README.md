# Investor profile library

35 profiles of real investors, one file each, for analytical simulation only. Every profile holds public, professional information with dated sources, and a few evaluation lenses that are labelled as analytical interpretation of that record. Nothing here is a statement by, or an endorsement from, any person listed, and nothing here says that anyone would invest in anything.

- Format and rules: [investor profile schema](../references/investor-profile-schema.md).
- Adding, updating and verifying a profile: [maintaining investors](../references/maintaining-investors.md).
- Validate the library: `startup-kit shark-tank profiles`. Show one profile's lenses: `startup-kit shark-tank profiles --id <id>`.

## How to read the tables

- **Seasons and status.** `current`: named in the line-up for the latest or announced season. `former`: a documented exit. `unconfirmed`: on the latest aired season, and the next line-up has not been announced. `dormant`: the programme has no announced future season. Panels change; the status is as of the date in the last column.
- **Confidence** limits what the simulation may say. `high` and `medium` allow a full evaluation. `low` runs in restricted mode: the reading mostly reflects the base read. See the schema for the minimums.
- **Lenses** are the labels of each profile's evaluation lenses. They describe what the documented record suggests the person examines in a business. They are interpretation, not fact.
- **Verified** is the date the profile's sources were last checked. A profile older than 18 months is treated as one level lower until it is re-verified.

Confidence across the library: 10 high, 23 medium, 2 low. Every profile was written by one researcher and then checked against its sources by a second, independent pass, which corrected or removed claims the sources did not support.

## Not profiled

The first version of the library generally leaves out, by decision and not for lack of sources:

- guest investors who appeared only occasionally, because a handful of appearances cannot support an evaluation profile;
- panel members who served a single season or a short tenure;
- members who joined in the most recent season of a programme whose next line-up is not yet announced;
- earlier panel members of the United Kingdom and Australian programmes beyond those listed above.

Exceptions are people whose business record outside the programme is substantial and well sourced, for example a guest who has since been named to a panel, or a one-season member who founded a large company.

When someone tries to add a person and the sources cannot meet the `low` minimum, list the person here with the date and the reason, so the attempt is not repeated blindly.

## Corrections and removal

If you are a person profiled here, or act for one, and something is wrong, out of date or should not be here, open an issue at https://github.com/jonasawasthi/startup-skills/issues or use the private contact route in [SECURITY.md](../../../SECURITY.md). State the file and the entry. A request to correct a fact is checked against the cited source and acted on; a request to remove a profile is honoured.

Anyone can report an error the same way. Maintainers re-verify a profile before changing it, as set out in [maintaining investors](../references/maintaining-investors.md).
