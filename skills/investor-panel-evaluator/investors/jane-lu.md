## Summary

Jane Lu founded the online fashion retailer Showpo in 2010 from her parents' garage in Sydney, after working in accounting [S3, S4, S5, S6]. She became sole owner in December 2011 [S5], and Inside Retail reported in 2017 that the company had grown without outside investment [S4]. Reported revenue rose from $2.6 million in 2014 [S6] to over $100 million a year according to documents reviewed by CNBC Make It in 2024 [S5]. She has said she grew the business through social media because traditional marketing was out of reach [S4, S5].
