# Peter Jones

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Peter Jones founded a telecoms group in 1998 built on a distribution model of his own design, and sold related companies to Vodafone and Westcoast Group [S6]. His investment group spans media, technology, consumer goods, retail, fashion and property; his site and the BBC give different counts of its holdings [S6, S1]. He founded the Peter Jones Foundation in 2005 and was appointed CBE in 2009 [S6, S1]. He co-owned Red Letter Days with Theo Paphitis from 2005 to 2017 [S11]. On his site he stresses honest forecasts, market research and backing the person as well as the idea [S7].
