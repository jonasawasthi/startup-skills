# Theo Paphitis

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Theo Paphitis is a retailer who bought Ryman from the receivers in the 1990s and later acquired other struggling retailers [S2, S4]. In 2015 he formed Theo Paphitis Retail Group, which owns Ryman, Robert Dyas, Boux Avenue and London Graphic Centre [S2, S6]. He launched Boux Avenue in 2011 and co-owned Red Letter Days with Peter Jones from 2005 to 2017 [S2]. He is acting as interim chief executive of Robert Dyas, which reported a widened loss for the year to March 2025 [S5]. In an interview republished on his site he puts leadership, cash and a business's reason to exist at the centre of how he judges businesses [S4].
