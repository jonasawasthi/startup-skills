# Naomi Simson

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-15.

## Summary

Naomi Simson founded the online experiences marketplace RedBalloon in 2001 and was its CEO until 2011 [S2, S9]. In 2017 she co-founded The Big Red Group, which acquired Adrenaline in 2018 [S2, S6]. Her site lists non-executive and independent director roles, including Big Red Group and Colonial First State [S2]. On her blog she argues that a proven business model comes before capital, and that customer acquisition funded only by outside money is not sustainable [S3]. She also writes that founder quality, including coachability, weighs heavily in her investment decisions [S4, S7].

- Shark Tank Australia: seasons 1–4 (2015–2018), former; not on the 2023 revival panel [S1, S9, S10].
