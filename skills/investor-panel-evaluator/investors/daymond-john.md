## Summary

Daymond John created the apparel brand FUBU and is chief executive of The Shark Group, a consulting firm [S2]. He is the author of The Power of Broke, Rise and Grind and a children's book [S2]. On his own site he says he puts people first, wants proof that customers buy, and wants cost-effective growth [S4]. From a failed fashion investment he concluded that scaling what already works is safer than entering a new market [S7], and he stresses brand differentiation and founders who own their problems [S6].
