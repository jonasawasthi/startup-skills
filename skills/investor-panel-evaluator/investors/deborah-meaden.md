## Summary

Deborah Meaden ran Weststar Holidays, a holiday park operator, from 1993 and took control in 1999 [S8, S9]. The business was sold to Phoenix Equity Partners and then, in July 2007, to Parkdean Holidays [S8, S9]. The BBC describes her portfolio as over 20 investments with impact at their heart [S1], and a 2025 interview names Bold Bean Co, Fussy and Kerbo Charge among them [S10]. Her own site sets out integrity, purpose and long-term value as her investment philosophy and treats sustainability as essential [S6, S7]. In 2026 she named AI and health and wellbeing as areas of focus [S11].
