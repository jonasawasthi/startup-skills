# Ritu Singh Vaidya

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-14.

## Summary

Ritu Singh Vaidya is Managing Director of United Traders Syndicate, the Toyota distributor in Nepal, a role recorded in 2017 and again in 2025 and 2026 [S6][S7][S10]. She also oversees heavy equipment distributorships within her family's VOITH group and became founding chair of the Nepal Automobile Importers and Manufacturers Association in 2025 [S9][S7]. Before the automobile business she ran a fashion design business and a spa [S9][S10]. In business interviews she puts profit and execution at the centre of a business, and says she now favours online and IT businesses that can reach beyond Nepal [S9][S10]. As NAIMA chair she has argued for after-sales readiness, supplier due diligence and consistent policy [S8][S9].

## Panel membership

- Shark Tank Nepal: seasons 1–2, current (season 2 announced on 28 September 2026 with the same five Sharks) [S2][S3][S4].
