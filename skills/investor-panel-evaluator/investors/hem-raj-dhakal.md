# Hem Raj Dhakal

> Profile for analytical simulation only. Public, professional information as of 2026-11-14.

## Summary

Hem Raj Dhakal co-founded IME, a Nepali remittance company, with his brother in 2001, and is listed by IME Group as its co-founder and managing director [S5][S6][S7]. The group grew from money transfer into banking, insurance, digital payments, hydropower, tourism, automobiles and retail [S6]. In 2019 he was also chairman of the group's general insurance and investment companies and founding chairman of Chandragiri Hills [S7], and in 2025 he chaired IME Limited's annual general meeting as its chairman [S8]. In a 2019 interview he described building trust and regulator confidence for a new financial service and criticised copy-cat entry into booming sectors [S7].

## Panel membership

- Shark Tank Nepal: seasons 1–2, current (season 2 announced on 28 September 2026 with the same five Sharks) [S2][S3][S4].
