# Interview guide: <segment>

> [!NOTE] Generate a tailored version with `startup-kit discovery interviews <spec>`, then lint it with `startup-kit discovery check <guide.md>`.

## Research objectives
- What we need to learn, and which decision it informs.

## Participants
- Criteria: people who dealt with <problem area> in the last 3 months.
- Recruitment: from observable signals, not friends and family.

## Script
1. Tell me about your role and what a normal week looks like.
2. Walk me through the last time you dealt with <problem area>.
3. What was the hardest part of that?
4. When did it last go wrong?
5. What happened next?
6. How do you handle this today?
7. What have you tried before?
8. Why did you stop using it?
9. How much do you spend today on tools, services or people for this?
10. Who decided on the current approach?
11. Tell me about the last time you paid for something to fix this.

## Close
- Who else deals with this that I should talk to?
- Commitment ask: a follow-up, an introduction, a pilot slot or a deposit.
