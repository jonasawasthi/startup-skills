# Growth and survival

## Three questions every plan must answer

1. **What is the one metric, and what is its growth rate?** (*Startup = Growth*)
2. **Default alive or default dead?** On current burn and growth assumptions, does revenue cover costs before the cash runs out? The financial model computes this. (*Default Alive or Default Dead?*)
3. **What if the next round doesn't happen?** Name the cuts and the ramen-profitable path. (*The Fatal Pinch*, *Ramen Profitable*)

## Presenting growth honestly

- Always show the base: "from 12 to 17 paying users (+42%)".
- Show the period and the cohort. Don't present cumulative charts as growth.
- Separate organic from paid growth.
- In multi-product ecosystems, report growth per product. Don't blend them.
