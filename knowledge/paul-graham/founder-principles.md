# Founder principles used in team assessment

| Dimension | Evidence to look for | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Relentless resourcefulness | Things shipped despite constraints, unconventional customer acquisition | Graham, *Relentlessly Resourceful* |
| Domain insight | Years inside the problem, a non-obvious observation | Friedman, *How to get startup ideas* |
| Customer access | Named channels to the first 10–100 customers | YC essential advice |
| Execution speed | Time from idea to launch, shipping cadence (git history is evidence) | Graham, *The Hardest Lessons*; YC "launch now" |
| Team durability | Equity clarity, commitment, history of working together | YC essential advice; Graham, *18 Mistakes* |
| Focus | Time split between building and selling, versus conferences and fundraising theatre | YC essential advice; Graham, *Maker's Schedule* |
| Integrity | Honest representation of progress (inflated claims count against the team) | Graham, *Mean People Fail*; Ralston's "don'ts" |

**Rule:** assess founders only on evidence provided or publicly verifiable. Never speculate about personal characteristics. Record gaps as *unknown*.
