# Arc PMF framework: three archetypes

| Archetype | What it looks like | Main obstacle | GTM implication | Examples cited |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hair on fire** | Urgent, obvious problem. Customers are already comparing solutions. The market is crowded | Differentiation | Clearly differentiated product + fast, aggressive sales | Wiz, Rippling |
| **Hard fact** | Pain accepted as "just how it is". Few people try to solve it | Inertia | Educate the market that it's solvable, then capture it | Square, HubSpot, Uber |
| **Future vision** | Creates a reality customers hadn't imagined | Disbelief | Find commercial traction along the way to fund a long journey; retain talent | Nvidia, OpenAI |

Sequoia notes that companies can move between archetypes and can straddle more than one.

## Use in Startup Skills

The idea evaluator classifies the idea's likely archetype, with reasoning, and checks that the go-to-market plan matches it. For example, a "hard fact" problem with a sales plan that assumes customers are already shopping is a mismatch.
