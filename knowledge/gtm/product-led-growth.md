# Product-led and developer-led growth

## Attributed

- **Definition (OpenView, who coined the term).** Product-led growth is a growth model in which product usage drives customer acquisition, retention and expansion. OpenView has wound down, so this file cites archived copies of its pages.
- **Conversion (Epstein, YC, 2023).** Getting visitors to a site is only the first step. Getting them to sign up, buy or learn more is a separate challenge, and good design is crucial to it.

## When PLG fits (original synthesis)

PLG is a channel *and* a product requirement. It fits only when:

1. users can **start without talking to anyone** (a self-serve sign-up, a free tier or a trial);
2. the time to first value is short (minutes or hours, not weeks);
3. the user can adopt the product, or has a clear path to a budget holder;
4. usage is **instrumented**, so activation and conversion are measurable.

If `product.self_serve` is false, the engine warns when PLG or developer-led growth is a primary channel.

**Developer-led growth** applies the same rules to developers. The channel is the documentation, quick-starts, SDKs, examples, a free tier and community answers. Leading indicators are time to first API call, weekly active projects and the share of accounts with more than one developer.

## PLG metrics

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Sign-up → activated | users reaching the activation event ÷ sign-ups |
| Time to value | median time from sign-up to the activation event |
| Free → paid | paying accounts ÷ activated accounts (cohorted) |
| Product-qualified leads | accounts meeting a usage threshold that signals buying intent |
| Expansion | net revenue retention, by cohort |
