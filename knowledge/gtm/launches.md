# Launches

## Attributed

- **Launch again and again (Mañalac, YC, 2019).** The library describes the talk as covering how startups should think about launching and why they should do it repeatedly. A launch is a recurring tool, not a single event.
- **Launch sooner (YC).** YC partners argue that founders should launch earlier than feels comfortable.
- **Product Hunt.** The Help Center explains how to post a product. Product Hunt reaches an audience of technology early adopters, so it suits developer tools, productivity software and consumer technology. It rarely suits local services, regulated products or non-technical audiences.

## Launch phases (original synthesis)

| Phase | Objective | Typical exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | Find and talk to the ICP; build a list of named prospects | N problem interviews done; list of committed early adopters |
| Waitlist | Measure intent; segment by need | Waitlist-to-interview and waitlist-to-deposit rates measured |
| Private beta | Deliver value by hand; instrument activation | Activation rate and first retention cohort measured |
| Public beta | Test channels at small scale | One channel with a readable CAC or conversion signal |
| Launch | Concentrated attention: press, community, partners | Activation holds at the higher volume |
| Post-launch | Repeatable acquisition; relaunch on milestones | Weekly growth in the one key metric |

For every phase, the plan lists the objective, audience, positioning angle, campaign, content calendar, channels, assets, partners, how feedback is collected, the activation plan and the follow-up.

## Rules

- **Launch to the atomic network first**, not to "everyone". A launch to the wrong audience produces vanity metrics.
- **Do not launch payments, regulated features or unready products to the public.** Product status gates what can be launched: only `live` products can be promoted as available. `built` means ready for a private beta at most.
- **Every launch is an experiment** with a stated hypothesis and success metric.
- **Plan the relaunch.** Each major feature, new segment or new geography is a new launch moment.
