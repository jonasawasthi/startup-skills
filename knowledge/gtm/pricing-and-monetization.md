# Pricing and monetisation

See also: [YC pricing](../yc/pricing.md), which covers the value anchor, alternative anchor and acquisition constraint, and [unit economics](../frameworks/unit-economics.md).

## Attributed

- **Pricing for B2B (Blomfield, YC, 2024).** The talk covers how to set a price and then justify it to customers.
- **Startup Pricing 101 (Hale, YC).** Covers pricing fundamentals and how price shapes acquisition strategy.
- **Usage-based pricing (OpenView, 2023).** OpenView's annual report tracked SaaS companies moving towards usage-based pricing, where customers pay for what they consume, and how to make the shift. OpenView has wound down, and its URLs now redirect, so this file cites the archived copy.

## Model menu (original synthesis)

| Model | Value metric examples | Fits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freemium | Seats, projects, usage caps | Viral or self-serve products; low marginal cost | Free users who never convert; support cost |
| Subscription | Per seat, per account, per tier | Recurring use | Churn; annual vs monthly mix |
| One-time purchase | Per product or course | Discrete outcomes (a course, a template) | No recurring revenue; needs repeat purchases or a catalogue |
| Transaction / commission | % of GMV, fixed fee per order | Marketplaces, payments | Disintermediation; take-rate pressure |
| Usage-based | API calls, minutes, GB | Developer and infrastructure products | Unpredictable bills; needs metering |
| Tiered | Features or limits per tier | Segments with different needs | Too many tiers |
| Enterprise | Contract value | Compliance-heavy buyers | Long cycles; procurement |
| Hybrid | Base fee plus usage or commission | Mixed segments | Complexity |

## Rules the GTM engine enforces

- Each price point has a `basis`: **researched** (backed by a `verified` or `reported` ledger entry from a price list, interview or dataset) or **hypothesis**. A "researched" price that cites an `assumption` entry is an error.
- A **competitor price** without evidence is an error. Pricing pages change, so cite them with an access date.
- Each hypothesis needs a **monetisation experiment**: what changes, for whom, the metric (conversion, ARPU, refund rate), the threshold and the duration.
- Commission models need a **take rate** between 0 and 1, and the marketplace section needs a disintermediation defence.
- A **value metric** must be named: the unit the customer pays for should grow with the value they get.

## Willingness-to-pay methods, strongest first

1. Completed payments at different price points (A/B test, cohort or geography).
2. Pre-orders, deposits or paid pilots.
3. Price at which deals were lost, with the reason recorded.
4. A Van Westendorp-style survey or a structured interview. These are directional only.
5. Competitor and substitute prices. These set an anchor, not a willingness to pay.
