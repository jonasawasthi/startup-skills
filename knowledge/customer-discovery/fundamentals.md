# Customer discovery fundamentals

See also: [GTM customer discovery](../gtm/customer-discovery.md) and [YC talking to users](../yc/talking-to-users.md).

## Attributed

- **A startup searches; a company executes (Blank, 2010).** Blank defines a startup as an organisation formed to *search* for a repeatable and scalable business model. Customer Development is his process for that search, and it runs alongside product development. Its four steps are commonly summarised as customer discovery, customer validation, customer creation and company building.
- **Lean start-up vs the business plan (Blank, HBR, 2013).** The article contrasts the lean approach with the traditional sequence: write a plan, pitch, build, launch, then sell hard. Blank argues that this sequence often ends in a fatal setback because its assumptions were never tested with customers.

## How the skill applies this (original synthesis)

1. **Every belief about customers is a hypothesis until tested outside the building.** The skill keeps hypotheses in a register linked to ledger evidence. A hypothesis is never treated as a fact because the founder is confident.
2. **Discovery comes before validation.** Discovery establishes who has the problem and how they handle it today. Validation tests whether they will change behaviour or pay. Interest found during discovery is not validation.
3. **Founders do the talking.** AI can plan, structure and analyse interviews. It cannot hold them or stand in for their results. The skill never writes an interview that did not happen.
4. **Stop, change or continue.** Each research round ends with a decision tied to evidence (persevere, pivot the segment, pivot the problem, or stop).
