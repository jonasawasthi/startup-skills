# Jobs to be done

## Attributed (Christensen Institute)

- A **job** is the progress a person is trying to make in a particular circumstance. People "hire" a product to make that progress, and "fire" it when something does the job better.
- Choices are shaped by **forces** that push people towards a change or hold them back. These forces come from circumstances, which change over time.
- Every job has **functional, social and emotional** dimensions.
- Demographics and product features miss the causal story. Understanding the circumstance reveals what a product really competes with, which is often something unexpected.

## How the skill applies this (original synthesis)

- **Write jobs as statements:** "When [circumstance], I want to [progress], so I can [outcome]." Every job statement is tagged `[job:<theme>]` in interview notes, linked to the interview it came from.
- **Ask for the story of the last time**, not an opinion: what triggered the search, what they tried, what they gave up, and who else was involved. The interview planner builds its questions on this sequence.
- **The competing alternatives include non-consumption.** Record "did nothing" and workarounds as alternatives (`[alternative:<theme>]`).
- **Segment by job and circumstance**, not only by demographics. Two segments that share a job can share one value proposition. One segment with two jobs may need two.
