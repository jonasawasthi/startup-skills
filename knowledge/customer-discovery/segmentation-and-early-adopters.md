# Segmentation and early adopters

See also: [positioning and beachheads](../gtm/positioning.md).

## Attributed

- **Earlyvangelists (Blank).** Blank's Customer Development writing introduced the "earlyvangelist", an early customer who will buy before the product is complete. The traits commonly cited from his book *The Four Steps to the Epiphany* are:
  - has the problem;
  - knows they have it;
  - is actively looking for a solution;
  - has assembled a makeshift fix;
  - has, or can get, a budget.

  This summary is attributed to the book's widely published description; the book was not re-read for this file.
- **Ideal customers (YC partners, 2022).** The partners weigh the pros and cons of selling to other startups as first customers.
- **Customer profiles (Strategyzer).** Strategyzer's Value Proposition Canvas profiles one segment at a time, by its jobs, pains and gains.

## How the skill applies this (original synthesis)

- **One profile per segment and per side.** In marketplaces and multi-sided platforms, supply and demand are separate segments, with separate interview guides, hypotheses and evidence counts. A finding from one side is never applied to the other.
- **Roles within a segment:**
  - *user* (uses it);
  - *buyer* (pays);
  - *decision-maker* (approves);
  - *influencers* (shape the choice);
  - *beneficiary* (gains from it).

  B2B segments almost always split these roles. B2C often merges them, but not always: in education a parent may pay, and in health a clinician may decide.
- **Describe segments with observable characteristics.** Use demographics for B2C and firmographics for B2B (size, sector, role, tools used), plus circumstances and triggers.
- **Prioritisation.** The engine scores each segment from 1 to 5 on pain, access, willingness to pay and reachable size, and every score needs a reason. Scores without evidence are labelled as judgement. Prioritisation decides which segment to *interview first*; it is not a market-size claim.
- **Early-adopter signals** are behaviours you can see before contact, such as a workaround, a public complaint, or a job post for the role that suffers. Recruit from those signals, not from friends.
