# 12 things about PMF (paraphrased)

1. Market matters most (Don Valentine). A great market supports many companies.
2. Market outweighs team (Andy Rachleff, who coined "product/market fit").
3. PMF can be pursued by a repeatable process of hypothesis testing (Rachleff).
4. Signs of fit: with it, customers buy faster than you can serve them (Andreessen).
5. PMF is hard to quantify (Eric Ries).
6. Founders tend to overestimate their fit (Alex Schultz).
7. Being first to PMF beats being first to market (Rachleff).
8. PMF isn't a single event, isn't always obvious, isn't permanent, and doesn't end competition (Ben Horowitz).
9. Premature scaling is a common cause of failure (Steve Blank; Startup Genome).
10. Find PMF before hardening the product (Jeff Lawson).
11. Hire after PMF, not before (Sam Altman).
12. Founders must choose a market before knowing if they'll find fit, so founder–market fit matters (Chris Dixon).

**Use:** the evaluator's "premature scaling" check (hiring plans, paid acquisition and a hardened product before level-3 PMF evidence) cites items 9–11.
