# Distribution

## Attributed

Andreessen's *The only thing that matters* (2007) argues that **market** matters most among team, product and market. In a great market, the market "pulls product out of the startup". The a16z PMF compilation adds Valentine's and Rachleff's views that the market outweighs the team.

## Distribution analysis (original synthesis)

For each customer segment, the evaluator answers:

1. **Where does the customer already look?** (communities, marketplaces, app stores, search, WhatsApp/Viber groups, associations)
2. **Who does the customer trust?** (peers, cooperatives, accountants, influencers, government)
3. **What is the cheapest repeatable channel**, and what is its CAC evidence?
4. **Is there a built-in loop?** (invites, content, creator audiences, payment receipts)
5. **What does the channel cost at 10× scale?** (paid channels saturate; partnerships take time)

Creator- and community-led products often have a **distribution advantage**: each creator brings an audience. The evaluator must check whether the product lets creators *move* their audience (portable) or *locks it in* (defensible but less attractive to creators).
