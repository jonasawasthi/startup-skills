# Marketplace metrics (paraphrased)

1. **Match rate**: how often buyers and sellers successfully connect.
2. **Market depth**: how much relevant supply is available to demand.
3. **Time to match**: how quickly supply and demand connect.
4. **Concentration**: the share of volume held by a few buyers or sellers.
5. **Take rate**: revenue as a share of GMV.
6. **Unit economics**: whether profit per transaction or per market improves with scale.
7. **Multi-tenanting**: users active on competing services.
8. **Switching / multi-homing costs**: how hard it is to leave.
9. **User retention cohorts**: newer cohorts retaining better suggests network effects.
10. **Core action retention cohorts**.
11. **Dollar and paid-user retention cohorts**.
12. **Retention by location**: mature markets retaining better is a positive sign.
13. **Power user curves**: engagement distribution over time.

The *Marketplace 100* is a16z's periodic ranking of consumer-facing marketplace startups by gross bookings. Use the current edition as a landscape source and record its publication date.
