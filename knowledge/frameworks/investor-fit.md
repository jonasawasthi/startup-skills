# Investor fit

## Research protocol (for any named investor, person or firm)

1. **Identity:** confirm exactly who the person or firm is. Disambiguate namesakes.
2. **Public investment record:** use primary sources first (the firm's portfolio page, the investor's own statements, company press releases, regulatory filings). Use secondary databases and news with dates. Record every finding in the ledger with a URL and access date.
3. **Thesis signals:** sectors, stages, geographies, cheque sizes and themes the investor has publicly stated or demonstrated. Separate *stated* thesis from *observed* behaviour.
4. **Recency:** activity in the last 24 months matters most. Investors' focus changes.
5. **Conflicts:** portfolio companies that compete with the startup.
6. **Strategic alignment:** where the investor's operating businesses or networks could genuinely help, or could conflict.
7. **Access path:** mutual connections, portfolio founders, accelerators, events. Never suggest misrepresentation or pressure tactics.

## Fit rating (qualitative, with evidence)

| Factor | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Stage | Invests at this stage | Only later stages |
| Sector / theme | Multiple relevant investments or stated thesis | No evidence |
| Geography | Invests in this region | Never has |
| Cheque size | Fits the round structure | Mismatch |
| Conflicts | None | Direct competitor in portfolio |
| Access | Warm path exists | Cold only |

## Hard rules

- Never imply that an investor is interested, has been approached, or is likely to invest without evidence.
- Use only public, professional information. No personal details, private addresses or speculation about personal life.
- Mark every unverifiable claim as such. Dated news is reported as "as of <date>".
- Outreach drafts must be truthful about traction and must not name-drop people who haven't agreed to it.
