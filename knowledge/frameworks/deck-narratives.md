# Deck structures

The 22 possible slides are a menu, not a checklist. Choose the smallest set that tells the story with the evidence available.

| Stage | Typical length | Must include | Usually omit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | 6–10 | Purpose, problem (evidence from interviews), insight, solution concept, why now, team, the ask/plan | Traction, financial outlook |
| Pre-seed | 8–12 | + product (what's built, with status), early users, market (bottom-up) | Detailed financials |
| Seed | 10–15 | + traction metrics, business model, competition, GTM, use of funds | Long roadmaps |
| Series A | 12–20 | + cohort retention, unit economics, GTM repeatability, financial outlook, hiring plan | Speculative products |
| Growth | 12–22 | + efficiency, segments, expansion, path to profitability | Concept-stage content |

## Audience adjustments

| Audience | Emphasis |
|---|---|
| VC | Size of outcome, growth, insight, team |
| Angel | Founder story, early proof, how the angel can help |
| Accelerator | Clarity, progress pace, founder–market fit |
| Corporate partner | Mutual value, integration, pilot design, risk |
| Government / grant | Public benefit, impact metrics, compliance, budget accountability |

## Short form (5–7 slides)

Cover → problem → solution/product → traction (or progress) → market → team → ask. Mark these slides `short: true` in the spec and build with `--variant short`.
