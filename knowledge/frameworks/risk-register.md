# Risk register

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| ID | R-01… |
| Category | market · product · technical · financial · regulatory · operational · distribution · team |
| Risk | A specific failure mode in one sentence |
| Likelihood | low / medium / high, with reasoning (qualitative; never a fake probability) |
| Impact | low / medium / high / existential |
| Evidence | Ledger IDs or "none yet" |
| Early warning signal | The observable metric that would show the risk materialising |
| Mitigation | A concrete action |
| Owner | Role |

## Prompts by category

- **Market:** is the pain urgent enough to change behaviour? Is the segment large enough? Is the timing right?
- **Product:** does v1 deliver the core job? Is retention evident?
- **Technical:** scaling, offline/connectivity, security, integration dependencies, key-person knowledge.
- **Financial:** runway versus milestones (default alive?), payment-fee margins, FX exposure.
- **Regulatory:** payments and e-money licensing, data protection, tax, foreign investment, content liability.
- **Operational:** support load, payouts, fraud, manual processes that won't scale.
- **Distribution:** dependence on one channel or platform (app store, social network), CAC drift.
- **Team:** founder conflict (Graham's *18 Mistakes*), missing skills, key-person risk.
