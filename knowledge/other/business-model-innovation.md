# Business model elements

Johnson, Christensen and Kagermann (HBR, 2008) describe a business model as four interlocking elements:

1. **Customer value proposition**: the job to be done for a target customer, and the offering that does it.
2. **Profit formula**: the revenue model, cost structure, margin model and resource velocity.
3. **Key resources**: the people, technology, partnerships and brand needed to deliver the proposition.
4. **Key processes**: the operational and managerial processes that make delivery repeatable.

Startup Skills uses these four as the skeleton of the *business-model* section in evaluations and proposals.

Blank (HBR, 2013) contrasts the lean start-up approach with traditional business planning: search for a repeatable business model through customer development and rapid iteration before executing a fixed plan. The proposal generator therefore separates **validated** elements from **hypotheses** in every business plan.
