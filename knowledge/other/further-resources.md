# Further resources

These are high-quality *directories* of founder material. They aren't summarised here because each contains hundreds of items. When a skill uses an article from one of them, record it in the startup's evidence ledger with its own URL and date. Don't cite the directory.

| Resource | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Stanford eCorner | Stanford's entrepreneurship talks and articles | Leadership, venture creation, case talks |
| MIT Martin Trust Center for Entrepreneurship | MIT's entrepreneurship centre (home of *Disciplined Entrepreneurship*) | Structured venture creation, beachhead market selection |
| First Round Review | Long-form operator interviews | Hiring, management, go-to-market tactics |
| Techstars *The Line* | Techstars' founder content | Accelerator preparation, fundraising |
| Stripe Atlas guides | Practical guides (SaaS economics, sales, incorporation) | Business models, US incorporation |
| YC Startup Library | YC essays, videos and talks | Everything early-stage |
| Paul Graham essays | Essays on startups and more | Principles and mental models |
