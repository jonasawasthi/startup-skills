# Network effects

NFX's *Network Effects Manual* lists 16 types, grouped as follows:

- **Direct:** physical, protocol, personal utility, personal, market networks
- **Hub-and-spoke**
- **Two-sided:** marketplace, platform, asymptotic marketplace
- **Expertise**
- **Data**
- **Tech performance**
- **"Social":** language, belief, bandwagon, tribal

NFX argues that network effects are the strongest form of digital defensibility. It cites its own study claiming that they account for about 70% of the value created in tech since 1994. That figure is NFX's estimate, so cite it as such. NFX also distinguishes network effects (defensibility) from virality (acquisition).

## Rules for Startup Skills

1. Name the **specific type**. "Network effects" alone is not an argument.
2. Show the **evidence**: retention, engagement or match rate improving with network density, by cohort or locality.
3. **Asymptotic marketplaces** (value plateaus after enough supply) and **multi-tenanting** weaken marketplace defensibility. Say so when they apply.
4. Creator–member ecosystems often have *personal/tribal* (community) effects that stay within a creator's audience. They can be strong locally and weak platform-wide.
