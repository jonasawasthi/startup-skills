# Business model validation and evidence quality

See also: [evidence standards](../frameworks/evidence-standards.md), [experimentation](../customer-discovery/experimentation.md), [problem-solution fit](../customer-discovery/problem-solution-fit.md).

## Attributed

- **Blank (2010)**: a startup searches for a repeatable and scalable business model; each element of the model starts as a hypothesis to test with customers.
- **Blank (HBR, 2013)**, **Ries (Lean Startup principles)**, **Bland and Osterwalder (*Testing Business Ideas*, 2019)** and **Blank's Investment Readiness Level (2013)** develop hypothesis testing and evidence-based progress measures; these were checked by URL and title only and are listed as further reading.

## Original synthesis: four states, never confused

| State | Means | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Conceptually coherent | All blocks described and consistent | None beyond the design itself |
| Supported by customer evidence | Customers confirm the problem and value | Interviews or behaviour backing segments or value propositions, and a supported customer-side assumption or problem-solution fit |
| Economically viable (demonstrated) | Money arrives and each transaction contributes | Paid revenue, and positive contribution from measured (verified or reported) inputs |
| Repeatable | It happens again without heroics | Repeat or retained payments and a measured acquisition cost |

## Evidence quality ladder

| Weaker | | Stronger |
|---|---|---|
| Founder opinion, desk research, analogies | Stated interest, surveys, waitlists (`stated`) | Observed behaviour (`observed`) → commitments, deposits, pre-orders (`committed`) → completed payments (`paid`) |

Rules the engine applies: a founder's opinion is never customer evidence; willingness-to-pay, pricing and revenue assumptions need committed or paid evidence; volume, retention, liquidity and margin assumptions need measured data; internal assumptions (costs, payments, regulation) need documents; contradicting evidence stays visible; and nothing is ever marked "validated": only *supported*, with the evidence listed.
