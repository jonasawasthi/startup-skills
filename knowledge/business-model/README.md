# Business model knowledge

Original summaries and frameworks used by the `business-model-designer` skill. They follow the [knowledge-base source policy](../README.md#source-policy). Each file's `sources:` front matter records each source's title, URL, author, date and verification level:

- **read-at-source**: the page text was read on the check date;
- **url-verified**: the URL and title were checked, but the full text (a book, a paywalled HBR article, or a page not re-read) was not.

Paywalled articles and books are never summarised beyond what their public pages show. `scripts/check_links.py` re-checks every URL and compares the live title with the recorded one.
