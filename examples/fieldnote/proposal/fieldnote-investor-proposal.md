# Executive summary

Fieldnote replaces paper delivery ledgers in agricultural cooperatives with an offline-first mobile app and a reconciliation console. Fourteen pilot cooperatives use it weekly, a company-reported figure [E-001], and five pay USD 40 per month, also reported [E-007].

> [!KEY] The core bet is that payment reconciliation, not data capture, is what cooperatives will pay for. Pre-sales in Q1 2027 test this directly.

## What we are asking for

We are preparing a seed round. The amount is not yet set and is listed in the missing-information checklist rather than estimated here.

# Problem statement

Managers spend an estimated 11 hours a month reconciling deliveries with payments [E-008]. The figure is an estimate from three pilot sites and will be re-measured.

- Records are captured on paper and re-typed
- Payments are matched by hand
  - Disputes are settled from memory
- Farmers wait weeks to be paid

# Market opportunity

Our bottom-up sizing multiplies the number of cooperatives by an assumed software budget [E-003, E-004]. Each layer inherits the weakest status of its inputs, so the whole sizing is an *assumption* until the budget is validated.

# Risks

1. **Willingness to pay.** Mitigation: annual pre-sales before hiring sales staff.
2. **Connectivity edge cases.** Mitigation: offline-first sync with conflict resolution, already live.
