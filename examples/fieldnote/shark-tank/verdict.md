# Fieldnote: simulated investment verdict

Basis: base read of 2026-11-15, ledger snapshot 2026-11-08, evaluations by the panel of 2026-11-15 ([panel-2026-11-15.md](panel-2026-11-15.md)).

## 1. Overall business assessment

Base read (15 dimensions): Strong 1 · Adequate 4 · Weak 0 · Unknown 10. Critical dimensions: D01 Adequate, D03 Adequate, D07 Unknown, D08 Unknown, D12 Unknown, D14 Adequate.

| Critical dimension | Rating | Confidence | Decisive evidence |

| D12 Financial sustainability | Unknown | medium | gap: no cash or burn recorded |

The evidence shows a problem that managers of cooperatives without an accountant describe in specific, recent terms [E-010, E-011, E-013], a live product that is used [E-002], and customers who have paid a year in advance [E-017]. It does not yet show what serving a cooperative costs, how cooperatives are won and at what cost, or how long the company's cash lasts: three of the six critical dimensions are Unknown. Nothing in the ledger points against the business, and nothing yet shows that it works economically. The founder-provided figures (fourteen weekly users and five monthly payers, [E-001, E-007]) are consistent with the verified evidence but have not been checked. In the panel, 2 of 3 members cannot form a view on current evidence and 1 of 3 holds a conditional stance; all three record gap concerns on D07, D08 and D12.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

- D03 and D06: three pilot cooperatives prepaid the annual plan, confirmed by a paid-invoice export [E-017]. This is the only verified payment evidence and the anchor of every member's top attraction.
- D01: three of four manager interviews describe a recent multi-day reconciliation, with spreadsheets and year-end accountants as the workarounds [E-010, E-011, E-013]; the segment without an in-house accountant is defined by the one interview that contradicts it [E-012].
- D05 and D14: offline capture is live and has logged 61,200 records [E-002], replacing paper slips that field agents describe losing [E-014, E-015].

## 3. Biggest weaknesses

No dimension is rated Weak. The weaknesses are gaps, listed in section 4. The closest thing to a weakness in the evidence is that every payer is a pilot cooperative [E-017, E-007] (founder-provided for the monthly payers), so demand outside a friendly group is untested.

## 4. Critical unanswered questions

- D07: is contribution per cooperative positive after support and onboarding cost, and which input is it most sensitive to?
- D08: which channel produced the paying cooperatives, and at what cost per cooperative?
- D12: how many months of runway exist, and does the plan reach the first renewal of the annual cohort inside them?

## 7. Conditions that would improve the case

- D07: eight weeks of logged support hours per cooperative and the cost of the last five onboardings, run through startup-kit unit-economics with measured churn.
- D08: each paying cooperative attributed to a channel, with the travel and time cost of each.
- D12: bank statements and a startup-kit model run showing runway to the next milestone.
- D03: renewals of the first annual cohort, or 90-day retention, from billing records.

## 8. Reasons an investor might decline

These are reasons the evaluations surface, each with its lens and evidence. They are not predictions.

- Runway is unknown (D12): raised through L3 in two evaluations, the cash-plan lens of one profile and the numbers lens of another; gap: no cash or burn in the ledger.
- The cost of serving a cooperative is unknown (D07): raised through L3 (command of the numbers) in one evaluation and as a base point in two; gap.
- The rules for holding member payment records are unresearched (D15): raised through L1 (licence and regulator confidence) in one evaluation; gap. Not legal advice.
- Scaling depends on onboarding effort of unknown size (D10): raised through L4 (technology with venture scale) and L4 (digital reach) in two evaluations; gap.
- All payers are pilot cooperatives [E-017, E-007] (founder-provided for E-007): a lens reading under L1 (model before capital) in one evaluation.
