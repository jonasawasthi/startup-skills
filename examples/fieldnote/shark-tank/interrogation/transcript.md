# Fieldnote: interrogation session 2026-11-15-a, readable transcript

*Fictional example. Fieldnote is an invented company. The founder's messages are scripted, fictional and illustrative: they are not a real conversation and not real research. The questions are raised through the documented lenses of real investors' sourced profiles; they are never words spoken by those people.*

This is the conversation that the [session file](2026-11-15-a.yaml) records, written out so the **turn contract** can be seen. Every assistant message ends with exactly one question, nothing follows it, and no other sentence in the message ends with a question mark. Each question asks for **one decisive unknown**, one fact or one figure: runway needs two figures, so cash and monthly spending are two turns, and a channel and its cost are two follow-ups. Before the question there are at most a status line and one line of acknowledgement. Facts the founder states are held as founder-provided in the session file and offered for the ledger once, at the debrief, never mid-session. The [debrief](2026-11-15-a-debrief.md) holds the assessment.

What to look for:

| Messages | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|
| 1 | Setup: simulation label, one paragraph of rules, then the first question last |
| 2 to 5 | Two figures asked as two questions; each new figure acknowledged in one line and held as founder-provided, not recorded |
| 6 to 7 | A request for a decision met with one boundary sentence, then the same pending question repeated (it adds no turn) |
| 8 to 9 | An honest "I don't know": accepted, no follow-up, the dimension stays Unknown |
| 10 to 15 | A vague answer, then a follow-up for the one missing anchor (the channel), then a second follow-up for the next anchor (its cost) |
| 16 to 17 | The founder asks to stop; the debrief message ends with the one recording question |

**The example stops here.** The founder's answer to the recording question is deliberately not scripted. A yes would run `bin/startup-kit shark-tank record examples/fieldnote/shark-tank/interrogation/2026-11-15-a.yaml --profile examples/fieldnote/startup-profile --confirmed`, which appends F1 to F3 to the ledger as `reported` entries with `source.type: founder`. The Fieldnote ledger is a tracked fixture that tests pin, so that command is never run in this repository. A no would record nothing, mark F1 to F3 "not recorded at the founder's request" in the debrief, set the session to closed, and send a closing message with no question.
