# Fieldnote (fictional example): base read

Stage: early-revenue · Business type: saas · Ambition: unknown · Ledger snapshot: 2026-11-08

Base read (15 dimensions): Strong 1 · Adequate 4 · Weak 0 · Unknown 10. Critical dimensions: D01 Adequate, D03 Adequate, D07 Unknown, D08 Unknown, D12 Unknown, D14 Adequate.

Ratings are Strong, Adequate, Weak or Unknown (not enough evidence to judge). They are never added up. For D11 and D15, Strong means the risk is low or well controlled.

## Dimensions

### D01 Problem severity: Adequate (confidence: medium) [critical]

Three of four cooperative-manager interviews describe a recent, specific month-end reconciliation that took days, with spreadsheets and year-end accountants as the workarounds [E-010, E-011, E-013], and a time-and-motion estimate puts the cost at about 11 hours a month per cooperative [E-008]. The interview evidence would allow Strong under the ceiling, but the sample is three incidents in one segment and the fourth manager, who employs an accountant, reports no problem [E-012]. That matches the Adequate anchor (small sample, one segment) rather than Strong; nothing points to Weak.

Decisive question: What does the problem cost a specific customer today, and what do they already do or pay about it?

- Verified: none
- Founder-provided or reported: [E-010] Customer interview I-01 (S1: Managers of mid-size dairy cooperatives; in person; participant M-01) (reported, interview); [E-011] Customer interview I-02 (S1: Managers of mid-size dairy cooperatives; video call; participant M-02) (reported, interview); [E-012] Customer interview I-03 (S1: Managers of mid-size dairy cooperatives; phone; participant M-03) (reported, interview); [E-013] Customer interview I-04 (S1: Managers of mid-size dairy cooperatives; in person; participant M-04) (reported, interview)
- Estimates: [E-008] Monthly payment-reconciliation time saved per cooperative (estimate)
- Assumptions: none
- Unverified or disputed: none
- Unknowns: How often payment errors reach members as complaints (experiment X2 has not run)
- Ceiling from the ledger: Strong
- Upgrade if: Six more manager interviews inside the narrowed segment (200 to 600 members, no in-house accountant), most describing a recent multi-day reconciliation, or complaint logs from five cooperatives showing payment-error complaints in most cycles
- Downgrade if: Further interviews in the segment where managers cannot recall a recent costly reconciliation
