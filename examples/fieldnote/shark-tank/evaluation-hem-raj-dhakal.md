## What attracts

On the documented record (S5, S7, S8), L2 reads adoption as a question of trust: the first customers of a new service have to believe a private company with something that matters to them. Here that is the record behind each member's pay, and three cooperatives paid a year in advance for it [E-017], with fourteen reported as using it weekly (founder-provided) [E-001] (A1). What L2 would look for next, repeat use and referrals, is not in the ledger.

On the documented record (S7), L3 asks what is new rather than copied. Fieldnote's offline capture is live [E-002] and replaces paper slips that two field agents describe losing [E-014, E-015] (A2). The comparison is with paper; whether other software already does this is unknown.

On the documented record (S7), L5 looks for founders who run the operation they describe. The capture system and the paid plans [E-002, E-017] show that much (A3); defined team roles do not appear in the ledger.

## Primary concerns

No dimension is rated Weak, so no concern rests on evidence against the business. The concerns are evidence gaps.

On the documented record (S6, S7), L1 puts permission first: whether the business holds, or has a dated path to, what regulators require. Fieldnote holds the records behind members' pay, and the ledger records no research on the rules that apply to them (C1). Not legal advice; confirm with qualified counsel.

On the documented record (S5, S7, S8), L2 asks how trust is earned with customers who have not used such a service before. The ledger shows that cooperatives pay, but not how they were won or whether they recommend it (C2).

Two critical dimensions that no lens of this profile covers are also open: contribution per cooperative (C3, D07) and runway (C4, D12).

## Recommendations

- M1 (D15): a sourced summary of the data-protection and payment-file rules, with plan, cost and timeline; hand to startup-research. Not legal advice; confirm with qualified counsel.
- M2 (D08): record how each new cooperative first heard of Fieldnote and log disputes raised through the app. Upgrade condition: attribution of each paying cooperative to a channel, with its cost.
- M3 (D04): a sourced competitor table including the status quo. Upgrade condition: the table plus switching evidence.
- M4 (D07, base): logged support and onboarding cost, run through startup-kit unit-economics.

## What would make the business more compelling under these lenses

A view needs the four open dimensions answered first. Under L1, a sourced statement of what the law requires for member payment records, and evidence that Fieldnote meets it, would close the dimension this profile weighs first. Under L2, channel records showing how cooperatives arrive, with a measurable share by referral, would show trust being earned rather than assumed. With D07 and D12 measured as well, most of the critical and primary set would be rated.
