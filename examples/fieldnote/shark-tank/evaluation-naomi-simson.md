## What attracts

On the documented record (S2, S3, S6), L1 asks first whether customers already buy. Fieldnote's answer is verified for three cooperatives that paid a year ahead [E-017], with five more reported as paying monthly (founder-provided) [E-007] (A1). All of them are pilots, which is the limit of this evidence under L1. On the same record, L1 also asks how cash moves: annual prepayment brings cash in before the service is delivered (A2).

On the documented record (S4, S7), L2 looks for leadership and responsiveness to evidence. The one thing the ledger shows on this lens is a priced test that was run and recorded [E-017] (A3).

On the documented record (S5), L4 looks at friction at each customer touchpoint. For field agents the friction point was the paper slip, which offline capture removes [E-002, E-014, E-015] (A4).

## Primary concerns

No dimension is rated Weak, so no concern rests on evidence against the business. The concerns are evidence gaps on the three critical dimensions this profile's lenses cover.

On the documented record (S4, S7), L3 expects a founder to know the figures that support the story. Burn and runway (C1) and contribution per cooperative (C2) are not in the ledger.

On the documented record (S3), L1 asks whether growth would be bought with outside money. The ledger does not show what a cooperative costs to win or how that cost is funded (C3).

L2 carries a self-described decline pattern about founder leadership. It does not apply: D14 is rated Adequate, and nothing in the ledger points against the team.

## Potential deal-breakers

None identified on current evidence. The decline pattern on L2 concerns founder leadership; D14 is rated Adequate, and nothing in the ledger points against the team.

## Recommendations

- M1 (D12): a monthly cash-flow statement from bank records and a startup-kit model run. Upgrade condition: runway to the next milestone shown from bank statements.
- M2 (D07): eight weeks of logged support and onboarding hours, turned into contribution per cooperative. Kill criterion: contribution that stays below zero at the current price.
- M3 (D03): the annual plan offered beyond the pilot group, recording who pays. Upgrade condition for D03 also needs renewals.
- M4 (D11): a map of the cooperative's month that marks every step a person does.

## What would make the business more compelling under these lenses

Under L3, being able to state burn, runway and contribution per cooperative from records would close the two numbers this lens expects first (D12, D07). Under L1, payment from cooperatives outside the pilot group, and acquisition paid for from subscription revenue, would show the model working before outside capital. Under L4, an onboarding that a system rather than a person carries would show growth without proportional hiring.
