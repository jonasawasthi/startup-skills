# Ottlebrook (fictional example): base read

Stage: early-revenue · Business type: hardware, consumer-goods · Ambition: growth · Ledger snapshot: 2026-11-10

Base read (15 dimensions): Strong 2 · Adequate 4 · Weak 5 · Unknown 4. Critical dimensions: D01 Adequate, D03 Strong, D07 Weak, D08 Adequate, D09 Weak, D11 Weak, D12 Weak, D14 Adequate, D15 Strong.

Ratings are Strong, Adequate, Weak or Unknown (not enough evidence to judge). They are never added up. For D11 and D15, Strong means the risk is low or well controlled.
