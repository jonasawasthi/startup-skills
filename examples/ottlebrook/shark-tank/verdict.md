# Ottlebrook: simulated investment verdict

Basis: base read of 2026-11-16, ledger snapshot 2026-11-10, evaluations by the panel of 2026-11-16 ([panel-2026-11-16.md](panel-2026-11-16.md)).

## 1. Overall business assessment

Base read (15 dimensions): Strong 2 · Adequate 4 · Weak 5 · Unknown 4. Critical dimensions: D01 Adequate, D03 Strong, D07 Weak, D08 Adequate, D09 Weak, D11 Weak, D12 Weak, D14 Adequate, D15 Strong.

| D15 Legal and regulatory risks | Strong | medium | E-015 verified |

The evidence separates the product from the business as it trades. The product is wanted: consumers bought 812 units in 29 weeks on the retailer's own data [E-007], the retailer paid and re-ordered [E-008], and DR-2 is certified for sale [E-015]. The business as it trades loses money on every unit: contribution through the retailer is AUD -3.36 per unit before any overhead [E-009], calculated from invoices, the signed agreement and the returns report. Fulfilling the re-order would need about AUD 70,200 of stock at landed cost [E-001, E-002, E-008] against AUD 96,000 of cash (founder-provided) [E-013]. All three panel members are not persuaded on current evidence, and all three record the concern on D07.

## 2. Strongest opportunities

- D03: sell-through of 812 units in 38 stores and a paid re-order of 900 units, both verified [E-007, E-008].
- D15: certification and registration complete [E-015].
- D10 and D07: a verified quote lowers the ex-factory price from AUD 61.80 to AUD 52.40 at a 3,000-unit order [E-001, E-017]. If freight, deductions, dispatch and the return rate stay as they are [E-002 to E-006], contribution through the retailer would be about AUD 6.51 per unit (a calculation from those verified inputs, not yet a ledger entry and not yet delivered).

## 3. Biggest weaknesses

- D07 and D06: the wholesale price after deductions is below the cost of delivering a unit; contribution AUD -3.36 [E-009].
- D09: one retailer, whose margin and deductions [E-003, E-004] remove the profit.
- D12: the re-order consumes most of the reported cash before the retailer pays on 60-day terms [E-003, E-008, E-013].
- D11: 5% heating-element faults [E-005] and a single manufacturer with no second source (founder-provided) [E-020].

## 4. Critical unanswered questions

None: no critical dimension is Unknown. The open questions that remain (D02, D04, D05, D13) are not in the critical set for a hardware and consumer-goods business at early revenue.

The judgements separate. The product has demand on verified evidence, so this is not a finding that nobody wants it. J1 is No for the business as it trades; for a changed form, J1 would start again as Not yet determinable, and J4 would become Yes, because the change has not been tested.

## 7. Conditions that would improve the case

- D07: a signed wholesale price or a delivered 3,000-unit order that gives positive contribution per unit, recalculated from invoices.
- D09: a second retail listing, or revised Retailer A terms, with positive contribution per unit.
- D12: a cash model from bank statements that stays positive through fulfilment of the re-order on profitable terms.
- D11: returns below 2% on the next shipment, and a second manufacturer quoted and sampled.

## 8. Reasons an investor might decline

Reasons the evaluations surface, each with its lens and evidence. They are not predictions.

- Loss per unit through the only main channel (D07), raised through store and franchise economics (L2) in one evaluation, profit as the test (L1) in another, and margin headroom for paid acquisition (L2) in the third [E-009].
- Retail terms that remove the profit (D09), raised through L2 (store economics) and L4 (after-sales and supplier credibility), and as a base point in one evaluation [E-003, E-004].
- Cash consumed by the re-order (D12), raised through L1 (profit as the test) in one evaluation and as a base point in two [E-008, E-013].
- Defect rate and single supplier (D11), raised through L4 (after-sales readiness) and L3 (repeatable operating model), and as a base point in one evaluation [E-005, E-020].
